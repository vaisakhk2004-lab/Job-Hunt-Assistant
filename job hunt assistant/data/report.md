### Job Analysis Report: Construction Analyst (REAC)

Below is the detailed analysis of the job opportunity based on the provided description.

---

#### 1. Job Title
**Construction Analyst (REAC)**

#### 2. Organization
**Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD)** – Assistant Secretary for Public and Indian Housing

#### 3. Job Location
**Remote** (Anywhere in the U.S.)

#### 4. Required Technical Skills
*   **Building Inspection:** Ability to evaluate residential or commercial building inspection plans and documents.
*   **Material/Method Knowledge:** Understanding of various construction materials and methods.
*   **Regulatory Compliance:** Ability to interpret statutory and regulatory requirements related to building standards.
*   **Reporting:** Proficiency in documenting inspection findings and reviewing property inspection reports.
*   **Asset Management:** Ability to inspect, review, and ensure assets meet development specifications.

#### 5. Required Experience
The position requires one year (52 weeks) of specialized experience equivalent to the grade level immediately below the target grade (GS-09 through GS-13). Key experience milestones include:
*   **GS-09:** Assisting in gathering information for inspections and documenting findings.
*   **GS-11:** Conducting inspections under supervision and reviewing property inspection reports.
*   **GS-12:** Applying established standards to evaluate physical conditions and recommending improvements.
*   **GS-13:** Inspecting/ensuring assets meet development specifications and interpreting complex regulatory requirements.

*Note: Experience may be gained in public, private, or volunteer sectors. Part-time work is prorated.*

#### 6. Educational Qualifications
Candidates may qualify through education or a combination of education and experience. Relevant fields of study include **architecture, engineering, or building construction.**

*   **GS-09:** Master’s degree, two years of graduate education, or a J.D./L.L.B.
*   **GS-11:** Ph.D. or three years of progressively higher-level graduate education.
*   **Documentation:** Official or unofficial college transcripts are mandatory to support education claims.

#### 7. Key Responsibilities
*   Evaluating residential or commercial building inspection plans and documents.
*   Assisting in or conducting property inspections to assess physical conditions.
*   Documenting inspection findings and drafting/reviewing formal inspection reports.
*   Ensuring that construction assets meet proposed development specifications.
*   Interpreting and applying statutory and regulatory requirements to building projects.
*   Recommending improvements based on established physical condition standards.
